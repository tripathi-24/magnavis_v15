\noindent\textbf{Kamal Kant Tripathi} is a Master of Technology student in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur. His thesis work, supervised by Prof.\ Saikat Ghosh and Prof.\ Amitangshu Pal, focuses on magnetic signature analysis and anomaly detection for static observatory monitoring using recurrent forecasting models and reproducible offline evaluation.
